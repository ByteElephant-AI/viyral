Frequently asked questions

How do lives work?

You have 4 lives. Losing a level costs one life, including when you replay a level for more stars. Winning costs nothing. A new life grows back every 20 minutes, even while the app is closed. You can also get a life right away for 100 coins.

How do I earn coins?

Spin the daily wheel once a day for coins. Seven days in a row and the seventh day pays double. You can also buy coin packs in the store.

What are mutations?

One-use powers you bring into a level from its intro screen. You can bring one per level, and a mutation is only used up when you actually use it. Your free daily gift mutation expires at midnight, so use it the same day.

I bought coins but didn't receive them.

Close and reopen the game with an internet connection; pending purchases are delivered when the app opens. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase and your Apple receipt, and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and choose the purchase you want to report.

Will my progress and coins move to a new phone?

Your progress, stars, coins and lives are stored on your device. If you delete the game or switch phones, they may not carry over, so please keep that in mind before deleting the app.

The music stopped. How do I get it back?

Check that the sound button on the title screen is on and that your phone's silent switch and volume are set the way you like. If it still doesn't play, restart the game and let us know.
