Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the VIYRAL app ("the game", "we") handles information. The short version: we do not collect any personal information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or anything else about you, and we do not sell or share personal data, because we do not have any.

What stays on your device

To let you play, the game saves your progress, stars, coins, mutations, lives, daily reward status and sound settings. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the game, this information is removed.

Purchases

The game offers optional in-app purchases of coins. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking. The only outside service involved is Apple's App Store, for downloads and purchases.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the game handles information, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
